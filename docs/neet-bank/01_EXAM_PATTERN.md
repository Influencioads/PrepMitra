## Notes

- **Botany and Zoology.** The 2026 bulletin lists Biology as one subject of 90 questions. The printed paper splits it
  into Botany (45) and Zoology (45), so PrepMitra uses two sections. Students see the same paper either way.
- **One timer.** There is no sectional timing. Students may move between sections at any time. The blueprint has no
  per-section `duration_min` and allows section switching.
- **Dropped or disputed questions** (bulletin): if more than one option is correct, +4 goes to anyone who marked any
  correct option. If all options are correct, +4 goes to everyone who attempted. If a question is dropped, everyone
  gets +4. PrepMitra's own questions are reviewed so this does not arise. If a student reports a faulty question,
  the admin review queue handles it.
- **Pattern history.** 2021 to 2024: 200 questions (per subject, Section A 35 plus Section B 15 with 10 to attempt),
  200 minutes. 2025 moved to 180 compulsory questions in 180 minutes, and 2026 kept it.
- **2026 exam dates** (for context only): first held 3 May 2026. NTA then held a re-examination on 21 June 2026
  (2:00 PM to 5:15 PM including formalities). Results were declared on 16 July 2026.
- **Check every cycle.** Re-read the next bulletin when NTA publishes it (usually February). If the pattern changes,
  edit `exam_patterns.json` and re-run the importer, which activates a new blueprint version.
