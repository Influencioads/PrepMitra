# Final audit (18 Nov 2026)

After Batch 10, every batch was searched for the problems found while writing Batches 1–10. A keyword sweep of Batches
1–9 gave 123 candidates; a separate reviewer read each one, and the real problems were rewritten by package authors,
then answered blind again (all matched).

## What was changed (Batches 1–9)

- **Outside the NMC 2026 syllabus (15 Physics, 1 Chemistry):** heat engines, Carnot engines, efficiency and refrigerator
  questions (the NMC Thermodynamics unit lists the first law, processes and the second law only); XeF₆/IF₇ shapes
  replaced with VSEPR molecules from the bonding chapter.
- **NCERT lines that are wrong in reality or out of date (Botany, Zoology, Chemistry):** Lupinus arcticus dormancy,
  Monascus called a yeast (5 questions), the Amazon's 20% of oxygen, the parietal false septum, ctenophores as radially
  symmetrical, 1100–1200 mL/min as the filtration rate, modern humans dated 75,000–10,000 years ago, the 20-billion-year
  universe, the 1997 Basmati patent, GEAC as "Approval" Committee (5 questions), the Patents Bill amendment, and a
  group 15/16 ionisation-enthalpy statement contradicted by NCERT's own data.
- **Counts that change over time:** biosphere reserves, national parks and sanctuaries; Amazon species numbers.

## Kept on purpose

- Questions that use XeF₂/XeF₄ only for VSEPR shapes (bonding chapter content).
- Curie temperature and Curie's law (NMC: effect of temperature on magnetic properties); the Curie–Weiss law was removed.
- Earthworm and cockroach mentioned in the Excretion, Breathing, Ecosystem or Animal Kingdom chapters (not anatomy).
- Zener diode, logic gates, LED, photodiode, solar cell and sol preparation: listed by the NMC syllabus although the
  rationalised NCERT dropped some of them.
