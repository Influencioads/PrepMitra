# Official Exam Patterns: Six Banking Exams (2026-27 cycle)

Verified on **5 November 2026**, directly from the official notification PDFs (text extracted from the PDFs, not
from coaching sites). The machine-readable version is [`exam_patterns.json`](exam_patterns.json); the validator and
the app should read marks from there, never from individual questions.

All six exams deduct **¼ of the marks assigned to a question** for each wrong answer, in both prelims and mains.
Unanswered questions carry no penalty. Scores across shifts are normalised (equi-percentile for IBPS).

## Summary

| Exam | Notification | Prelims | Mains | Final stage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| IBPS PO | CRP PO/MT-XVI | 100 Q, 100 marks, 60 min | 170 Q, 200 marks, 160 min + descriptive 25 marks, 30 min | Personality test + interview (80:20) |
| IBPS Clerk | CRP CSA-XVI | 100 Q, 100 marks, 60 min | 160 Q, 200 marks, 125 min | None; Mains score only |
| IBPS RRB Officer Scale-I | CRP RRBs-XV | 80 Q, 80 marks, 45 min | 200 Q, 200 marks, 120 min | Interview (80:20) |
| IBPS RRB Office Assistant | CRP RRBs-XV | 80 Q, 80 marks, 45 min | 200 Q, 200 marks, 120 min | None; Mains score only |
| SBI PO | CRPD/PO/2026-27/09 | 100 Q, 100 marks, 60 min | 170 Q, 200 marks, 180 min + descriptive 30 marks, 30 min | Psychometric + group exercise + interview |
| SBI Clerk (JA) | CRPD/CR/2026-27/17 | 100 Q, 100 marks, 60 min | 190 Q, 200 marks, 160 min | Local language test (qualifying) |

Every section in every exam is **separately timed** in the 2026-27 notifications.

## 1. IBPS PO/MT (CRP PO/MT-XVI)

- **Notification:** [Detailed Notification, 30.06.2026](https://www.ibps.in/wp-content/uploads/Detailed-Notification_CRP-PO-XVI_Final_V1_30.06.2026.pdf) (vacancies of 2027-28)
- **Vacancies:** 6,715 in Annexure I (Indian Bank, UCO Bank and Union Bank of India marked "NR", not reported). 11 participating public sector banks.
- **Age:** 20 to 30 years as on 01.07.2026 (born 02.07.1996 to 01.07.2006). Upper-age relaxation: SC/ST 5 years, OBC (NCL) 3 years, PwBD 10 years, ex-servicemen 5 years.
- **Education:** graduation in any discipline (as on 21.07.2026).
- **Fee:** ₹850; ₹175 for SC/ST/PwBD.
- **Dates:** registration 01.07.2026 to 21.07.2026. Prelims on 22 and 23 August 2026 and Mains on 4 October 2026 (IBPS Calendar 2026-27). Interviews November/December 2026; provisional allotment January 2027.
- **Selection:** Prelims (qualifying, sectional cut-offs) → Mains (objective + descriptive) → Personality Test (self-report, non-qualifying but mandatory) → Interview (100 marks). Final merit: Mains and interview in the ratio **80:20**.

**Prelims** (60 minutes):

| Section | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| English Language | 30 | 30 | 20 min |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 35 | **30** | 20 min |
| Reasoning Ability | 35 | **40** | 20 min |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** | **60 min** |

**Mains** (objective 160 minutes + descriptive 30 minutes):

| Section | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Reasoning | 40 | 60 | 45 min |
| General/ Economy/ Banking Awareness/ Digital/ Financial Awareness including RBI circulars | 50 | 60 | 35 min |
| English Language | 40 | 20 | 35 min |
| Data Analysis & Interpretation | 40 | 60 | 45 min |
| **Total** | **170** | **200** | **160 min** |
| Descriptive (Essay and Comprehension) | 2 | 25 | 30 min |

## 3 and 4. IBPS RRB Officer Scale-I and Office Assistant (CRP RRBs-XV)

- **Notification:** [CRP RRBs-XV notification (revised)](https://www.ibps.in/wp-content/uploads/CRP-RRBs-XV-notification_Revised.pdf), plus corrigenda and an [updated Annexure-I (25.09.2026)](https://www.ibps.in/wp-content/uploads/Annexure-I_updated_25.09.2026.pdf) for vacancies. An [extension corrigendum](https://www.ibps.in/wp-content/uploads/Approved_Corrigendum-for-Extension_CRP-RRBs-XV-002.pdf) changed the registration window.
- **Age (as on 01.09.2026):** Office Assistant 18 to 28 years; Officer Scale-I above 18 and below 30 years.
- **Education:** bachelor's degree in any discipline, plus proficiency in the local language prescribed by the RRB. For Officer Scale-I, preference goes to degrees in agriculture, horticulture, forestry, animal husbandry and related fields. For Office Assistant, working knowledge of computers is desirable.
- **Dates:** registration from 01.09.2026 (extended by corrigendum). Officer Scale-I prelims 21 and 22 November 2026, mains 20 December 2026. Office Assistant prelims 6, 12 and 13 December 2026, mains 30 January 2027 (IBPS Calendar). Officer interviews January/February 2027, coordinated by Nodal RRBs with NABARD.
- **Selection:** Officer Scale-I: Prelims → Mains → Interview (100 marks), with online exam and interview in the ratio **80:20**. Office Assistant: Prelims → Mains, **no interview**.

## Findings that affect PrepMitra

1. **Fix the app's IBPS PO prelims blueprint.** In `backend/app/seed.py`, IBPS PO prelims gives Quant and Reasoning 35 marks each. The official 2026 pattern is **Quant 30 marks and Reasoning 40 marks**, with the same 35 questions each.
2. **Fix the app's RRB prelims timing.** `seed.py` uses one combined 45-minute timer for both RRB prelims. CRP RRBs-XV now times each section separately: **Reasoning 25 minutes and Quant 20 minutes**.
3. **Mains are not in the app yet.** All GA, Banking and Computer questions are tested only in Mains, so 4,500 of the 10,000 questions need Mains blueprints before students can see them in mocks.
4. **Computer Knowledge is only in four exams.** It is tested in the RRB Officer Scale-I and Office Assistant Mains (its own 40-question section) and in the SBI PO and SBI Clerk Mains (combined with Reasoning). **IBPS PO Mains 2026 names its section just "Reasoning"**, and IBPS Clerk Mains has no computer section, so computer questions are not mapped to those two.
5. **Per-question marks are fractional in several sections.** For example, IBPS PO prelims Quant is 30/35 = 0.857 marks per question, IBPS PO Mains English is 0.5, and RRB Mains Reasoning is 1.25. The notifications state only section totals, so PrepMitra should score by section (max marks ÷ questions, with a penalty of ¼ of that) and not store a fixed mark on each question.
6. **The real exams use five options (A to E); PrepMitra uses four (decided 5 Nov 2026).** For question types with a fixed set of five answers (inequality, syllogism, quantity comparison, data sufficiency), [README.md](README.md) sets out which answer is dropped.
