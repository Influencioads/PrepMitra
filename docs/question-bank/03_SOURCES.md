## D. Previous-year papers: copyright position

- **IBPS and SBI do not publish previous-year question papers** for public reuse. Their notifications and
  "Information Handouts" include only a few illustrative questions.
- Question banks on coaching sites (Adda247/Bankersadda, Oliveboard, Testbook, Career Power, Guidely, PracticeMock)
  are copyrighted, and their "previous-year papers" are memory-based reconstructions, not official papers.
- **Therefore every question in this bank is labelled `Original`**, written to match the official pattern and
  difficulty. No question claims to have appeared in an exam. The schema still has `Official PYQ` and `Licensed`
  types so that content can be added later if PrepMitra gets a licence; the validator then requires a source URL and a
  licence note.
- **Not done:** no scraping and no copying of coaching-site questions.

## E. What could not be verified

- Exact registration end date after the RRB-XV extension, and the CSA-XVI vacancy changes in the 29.09.2026
  corrigendum. Neither affects the exam patterns.
- The RBI's own wording on the LCR inflow cap. The LCR sample question is written so that its answer does not depend on
  the cap.
- Topic weights in `taxonomy.json` are **estimates** based on section sizes and the analyses in section C. They are
  not measured counts from official papers, because no official papers exist.
